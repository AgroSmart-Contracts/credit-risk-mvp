Methodology

  • Group raw average = Σ(score × weight) / Σ(filled weights); blanks excluded
  • Coverage fraction = filled weight ÷ possible weight
  • Shrink = coverage × raw + (1 − coverage) × 50
  • TM platform trust = +3 flat after shrink (platform counterparties + TM affiliates)
  • Customs enrichment fills annual volume (MT), yearly FOB sales proxy, monthly FOB, route diversity, online verifiability (less-authoritative = 55 when found in Veritrade/Datasur)
